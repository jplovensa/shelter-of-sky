# Shelter of Sky

A playable Three.js architectural model based on **echo 3 - 17 FEB1.skp**, with the **Modern Tropical** material direction from **To Breathe — FG Signature Catalogue**, Shelter of Sky interior page 11 and exterior page 6.

## Explore

- Orbit, furnished floor plan, and first-person walkthrough.
- Seven architectural viewpoints: whole retreat, living/dining, kitchen/bar, both bedrooms, bath/dressing, terrace.
- Daylight, golden hour and dusk, with interior lighting.
- Section height, original/Modern Tropical finishes, garden visibility.
- Guided tour, view capture and touch movement controls.
- Original catalogue moodboard and exterior material sheet, available within the viewer.

Visible source architecture, furnishings, fixtures, transforms and textures are converted from the supplied SketchUp model. Source hidden elements remain hidden. The viewer retains the original three-pavilion architecture and site with a refined pool proposal. The modern tropical finish study adds a green zellige backsplash, light terrazzo island top, teal accents and planting; it is a design visualization, not a construction specification.

## Run locally

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8771 --directory dist
```

Open http://localhost:8771. No build, external CDN, API key or installation is required for playback. The complete interiors load as an optimized GLB (~26 MB); allow time on mobile connections. Desktop with a modern WebGL browser is recommended for the full model.

## Source and materials

The source conversion contains 435,870 unique triangles, 77 materials and 50 textures. The source site extent is 23 × 13 m. Coordinates are transformed from SketchUp inches/Z-up to metres/Y-up and centered for browser navigation. Original scale and component placement are retained.

Modern Tropical interior: light grey microcement, board-finish concrete, natural teak, dark lava stone, glossy green zellige, light terrazzo, clear fluted glass, matte black metal and rattan; teal, clay and moss accents. Exterior: warm beige stucco, pale travertine, natural teak, matte black frames, light concrete, sand paving and dark charcoal shingles.

The catalogue pages are original Fjäll/SECHA artwork supplied by the project owner. The superseded generated concept board is not used in the published application.

Physical textures and HDR environment from Poly Haven (CC0), credits in `dist/assets/realism/credits.json`. Additional tile, stone and woven material maps are generated by `tools/build-surfaces.py`. Three.js and meshoptimizer retain their bundled licenses in `dist/vendor/`. Project architecture and catalogue artwork remain with their respective rights holders; public repository visibility does not grant a license to those assets.

## Conversion and reproducibility

`tools/convert_skp.py` uses the installed SketchUp C API on macOS (SketchUp 2025 default library path, configurable with `--sdk`). `tools/package_model.py` resamples source textures to 1024 px and preserves transparency. `tools/prepare-model.mjs` uses glTF Transform 4.5.1 and meshoptimizer 1.3.0 (`OPTIMIZER_ROOT` points to their node_modules folder), separates interactive doors, batches the static geometry, deduplicates and compresses it. `tools/build-surfaces.py` requires Pillow. The original SKP is kept outside the repository.

## Navigation limits

Walk mode checks the source geometry for obstacles at body and eye height. Q/E changes height freely; gravity, automatic stairs and game physics are not simulated. Floor-plan double-click starts walking at the selected surface. Material substitutions are a catalogue-led proposal; source furniture shapes are preserved rather than claimed to match every catalogue photograph.

## Interactive doors and refined furniture

Four original door assemblies are separated from the static model: social front, social rear, east bedroom and west bedroom. In Walk mode, approach a door and click its leaf, click the nearby prompt, or press **F**. Doors animate and their moving geometry participates in movement checks. `Open all doors` is available under Architecture & materials. Doors are a visual interaction study; hardware, swing clearances and detailed construction remain design decisions.

The living sofa is rebuilt with a teak platform, tapered feet, separate seats, loose backs and accent cushions. Coffee tables have pedestal structures and softened tops. Three new counter stools include tapered legs, foot rails, woven backs and curved teak rails. Dining furniture uses softened edges and structural legs. Bedroom source furniture retains its detailed forms and receives the coordinated material treatment. Replaced source nodes are recorded in `docs/source-edit-manifest.json`.

The source shallow pool receives a water surface in a refined 6.0 × 2.0 m rounded rectangular basin: animated normals, sun highlights, alpha blending and planar reflections using the official Three.js Water renderer. Water ripples can be paused. Inter is used throughout and the Fjäll Group logo has a transparent background. Surrounding trees have been removed.

Two complete bathroom proposals are added at the rear of the bedroom pavilions: floating teak vanities, vessel basins, curved taps, backlit round mirrors, shaped toilets, lava-stone shower trays, rain showers, green tile and clear shower screens. These newly furnished wet areas are an inferred interior study within the source footprint and require layout/plumbing confirmation before construction.

The refined pool has a centered 6.0 × 2.0 m water surface, 220 mm corner radius, 240 mm coping and a 1.10 m basin study. A material cutout removes the old source deck within the proposed basin; the new walls and floor define the volume. This dimensional proposal needs site, structural and circulation confirmation.

Bedroom refinement: solid runner overlays removed from both beds; original mattress, duvet and pillow geometry is preserved.

Geometry quality pass: source normals rebuilt with a 45-degree crease threshold, keeping architectural corners crisp while smoothing cloth, rounded furniture and curved roof surfaces. Geometry is welded after normal generation and batch compression. The renderer uses up to 2x pixel density, 4-sample offscreen antialiasing, anisotropic textures and subtle fabric normal strength. The dining set is rebuilt as a 1.80 × 0.90 m teak table with six curved rattan chairs.
